What the Toolbox GPT actually does

Think of the Toolbox as the friendly front desk for the whole Glee‑fully suite. It doesn’t replace the other GPTs — it makes them easier to find, understand, and reuse.

Explains the ecosystem

The Toolbox walks you through the Trunk → Tools → Tool‑ettes → Functions structure so you always know where you are — and where to go next.

Find a fitting helper

Browse by task — “plan a trip,” “organize bills,” “log my books” — and read the Tool or Tool‑ette description and launch status before trying it.

A concierge concept for the suite

The external Toolbox GPT is intended to help visitors choose among the suite's Tools and Tool‑ettes. This site does not verify its recommendations, persistent memory, or context sharing with other GPTs.

Why start here instead of vanilla ChatGPT?

Generic ChatGPT is powerful but general‑purpose. The Toolbox is tuned around one person’s life — Glee’s — and by extension, yours.

Using related catalog pages

The Toolbox catalog shows how the seven branches relate. Browse those pages and bring your own context when you choose an external GPT; automatic routing and shared records are not verified.

Template‑first by design

Instead of loose chat, it leans on ready‑to‑copy tables, trackers, and checklists you can drop into docs, sheets, or Notion.

Dialable tone & “BLEED GLEE” mode

You can ask the Toolbox to turn the charm up (BLEED GLEE) or keep things neutral and professional — no prompt‑engineering required.

How Toolbox, Tools, and Tool‑ettes fit together

The Toolbox is the trunk of the tree. Tools are the big branches, and Tool‑ettes are the twigs and leaves where everyday work happens.

Toolbox · Trunk · #00

Lives at the very top of the system. It stays high‑level: explaining structure, helping you choose a branch, and opening GPTs with a shared vocabulary.

Tools · Branches · #01–#07

Each Tool is a themed hub (careers, collections, food, travel, life admin, health, identity). Their web pages dive deeper into use‑cases and Tool‑ette families.

Tool‑ettes · Twigs & leaves

Specialized GPTs like Resume Builder or Book Tracker live under each Tool. The Toolbox mentions them at a high level; their full stories live on the Tool pages.
