## Classification rules

- **Executable in release:** an accepted correction carried into the release, or a remaining check/action within the authorized release. Local completion is stated separately from remote/live completion.
- **Completed proposal delivery:** the requested reviewable option, policy, or design was delivered and accepted as a deliverable. Adoption is a separate decision.
- **Separately deferred / not adopted:** no implementation or settings change is included in this release. It is not an unfinished action silently assigned to the release PM.
- **External human evidence:** the fact requires provider, product-owner, participant, assistive-technology, or field evidence that the local source and automation cannot supply. It remains unknown; no unsupported capability or conformance claim is restored.

Mixed rows identify both the completed deliverable and the remaining boundary. The categories distinguish release obligations from separately scoped proposals, rather than marking every limitation as a release blocker or pretending all limitations are solved.
