A note from Jamie | September 29, 2026

The ideas are staying.
The platform is changing.

Custom GPTs helped me turn ideas into useful companions for life, work and wonder. Building them was a remarkable learning chapter. Now I am working on what comes next.

Platform transition underway

OpenAI has announced that Custom GPTs are scheduled to retire on December 11, 2026. I am aware of the change and actively working to preserve and replatform the concepts, solutions and systems built around them.

Browse the original catalogMeet the FoundRy

Carrying the useful parts forward

The learning, methods and care belong to the work, even when its platform changes.

Preserve the learning

I am reviewing instructions, reference material, prototypes and unfinished ideas alongside the GPTs that reached publication. Useful work deserves a future even if it never became a finished GPT.

Build reusable skills

I am shaping focused Agent Skills that can work together. Related capabilities may be consolidated into a coherent product instead of keeping every old GPT boundary.

Package and test

Related skills can become plugins for supported platforms. Each replacement needs testing, working integrations where required, and a clear way for its intended users to access it.

What this means when you browse

  • The catalog remains a record of the original ideas. Descriptions and names help you find the work; they do not certify a finished replacement.
  • Existing GPT links are legacy destinations. OpenAI says existing GPTs remain usable until their applicable retirement date, subject to access and workspace permissions. This site has not independently verified every destination.
  • A replacement is ready only when its page says so. I will share a new destination after review, testing and access checks. There is no blanket promise that every concept will migrate unchanged or on the same schedule.
  • Your past conversations do not automatically move. OpenAI's migration workflow does not transfer GPT conversations or automatically give existing users access to a replacement plugin.

This transition concerns Custom GPTs in ChatGPT. It does not mean that every AI agent, bot, skill or Glee-fully web tool is being retired. The Arcade remains its own web experience.

Read OpenAI's retirement and migration FAQ. Some qualified Enterprise workspaces have an approved later date; follow the notice for your account or workspace. Dates and platform options may change.

Status as of September 29, 2026: migration work is underway. This page announces the direction and the transition, not a completed portfolio migration.
