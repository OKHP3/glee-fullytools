---
name: Tool-ette evidence contract
description: Evidence boundary for externally hosted Tool-ettes and the public catalog's verification language.
---

An HTTP-successful external GPT URL proves only that a destination responded at
review time. It does not prove the displayed identity, ownership, configuration,
knowledge, actions, conversation behavior, privacy handling, or safety
boundaries. Keep those claims separate from the static catalog's authored
description.

**Why:** The public hub can control its own copy and routing, but it cannot
observe private Custom GPT configuration or promise third-party availability.

**How to apply:** Before a Tool-ette is called verified, require owner-supplied
identity/configuration evidence plus representative behavior and safety
evidence. Otherwise use partial, unavailable, construction, or blocked states
and withhold misleading launch CTAs.
